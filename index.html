Dono Runtime Backup

Dono Runtime Backup is a private-use utility that creates encrypted off-host backups of the owner's Dono Runtime staging environment and stores those backups in the owner's Google Drive.

Purpose

The application uses Google Drive access only to upload, verify, list, retrieve for recovery, and manage encrypted backup files in a dedicated backup location controlled by the account owner.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Google Drive information is used only for backup and recovery operations requested by the account owner.

The application does not use Google Drive information for advertising, marketing, or profiling, and does not sell or rent it to third parties.

Backups are retained according to the owner's backup policy. The owner can delete stored backups and revoke the application's Google Account access at any time.

This policy may be updated if the application's use of Google Drive changes.
