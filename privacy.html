Privacy Policy

Dono Runtime Backup is a private-use utility for creating encrypted backups and storing them in the owner's Google Drive.

Google Drive access

The application requests Google Drive access only to upload, verify, list, and manage backup files in the owner's Drive.

Data use

Drive data is used only for backup and recovery. The application does not sell data, use it for advertising, or share Drive contents with third parties.

Storage and security

Backup contents are encrypted before they are stored off-host. The application is operated only for the account owner's personal use.

Control

The account owner controls the Google account and may revoke the application's access through Google Account permissions.
